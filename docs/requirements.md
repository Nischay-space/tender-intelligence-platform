# Tender Intelligence Platform

## Product Requirements Document

# Problem Statement

Organizations looking for government and public procurement opportunities often need to monitor multiple tender portals manually.

Each portal has a different interface, search mechanism, and data format. This makes tracking relevant tenders time-consuming, repetitive, and error-prone.

There is no unified platform that continuously collects, normalizes, stores, and presents tender information from multiple sources in a searchable and extensible manner.

The Tender Intelligence Platform aims to solve this problem by automating data collection, maintaining a centralized database of tenders, and providing APIs, dashboards, and AI-assisted insights for users.

## Project Goals
- This product acts as a centralized platform for tenders from different        organisations.
- This product should be easy to use and interactive.
- This product targets the organizations that need Tender information.
- This product offers a smooth workflow for tender extraction.
- This products tends to become a platform for diverse tender handling.
- The product would provide efficient services so one should use it to enhance their productivity.
- The product should follow the principles of SRP and SOC have modularity and reliable data.
